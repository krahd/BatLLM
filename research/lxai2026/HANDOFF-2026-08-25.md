## 1024-token deliberation budget control — COMPLETE

Canonical: `controls/DELIBERATION-BUDGET-CONTROL.md`  
Raw: `controls/deliberation_budget/20260825T185106Z/`

Original 256 deliberative arm:

- 238/256 = 93.0% raw correct;
- 241/256 valid;
- 15 strict final-line extraction failures.

1024 control:

- **253/256 = 98.8% correct**;
- **256/256 valid**;
- **0 extraction failures**;
- all end with Ollama `done_reason=stop`;
- **15/15** old extraction failures become valid and correct;
- **14/15** old failed responses are exact text prefixes of the corresponding longer 1024 completion;
- **240/241** old successful responses are byte-for-byte unchanged;
- **241/241** old successful final commands remain unchanged.

Interpretation: the old 15 failures were fixed-output-budget truncations to a very high evidentiary standard.

### Language-conditioned budget artefact

Qwen30 at 256:

- tuteo mean chars 366.6; 61/64 correct; 3 extraction failures;
- voseo mean chars 472.9; 51/64 correct; 12 extraction failures.

Qwen30 at 1024:

- tuteo 64/64 correct;
- voseo 63/64 correct;
- zero extraction failures.

Pooled Mistral24 + Qwen30 at 1024: 127/128 tuteo versus 126/128 voseo. The earlier raw deliberative voseo deficit is therefore almost entirely a budget-truncation artefact rather than an action-selection competence gap.

Do not claim this general mechanism is novel: Goyal & Ray, *Mind the Cap: Output-Budget Regimes Change the Measured Multilingual Reasoning Gap* (arXiv:2608.04160, 2026), is direct prior art. The narrower contribution is a within-Spanish tuteo/voseo instance inside executable-agent evaluation.
