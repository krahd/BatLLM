## CURRENTLY RUNNING: 1024-token budget control

Runner:

`run_deliberation_budget_control.py`

Analysis:

`analyze_deliberation_budget_control.py`

Why it exists:

- the clean factorial has 15 strict final-line extraction failures in the 256-token deliberative arm;
- failed responses are unusually long and strongly consistent with budget exhaustion;
- the external audit reports all 15 in Qwen30, with longer Rioplatense scratchpads than tuteo and more failures in voseo/order cells;
- the original runner did not retain `done_reason` or `eval_count`, so truncation was suggestive but not formally proven.

Control design:

- rerun exactly the 256 deliberative clean-factorial cells;
- same models/languages/orders/cases/clauses/temp/seed;
- `num_predict=1024`;
- store full response and score the strict `FINAL:` line;
- additionally retain Ollama `done`, `done_reason`, `eval_count`, and `prompt_eval_count`;
- pair each row to the original 256-token deliberative row.

The analysis writes:

- `controls/DELIBERATION-BUDGET-CONTROL.md`
- `controls/DELIBERATION-BUDGET-CONTROL.json`

and tests:

- extraction failures at 256 vs 1024;
- raw and valid-conditional correctness by model/language;
- old-wrong/new-correct and old-extraction-failure/new-correct repair;
- done reasons and evaluation counts;
- response lengths;
- whether each old failed 256 response is an **exact prefix** of the corresponding 1024 response, which would be especially strong evidence of truncation.

A budget-only direct arm is unnecessary because direct responses are at most four characters and the 24-token ceiling never binds.

## Prior-art boundary added after the budget critique

Do not claim novelty for multilingual gaps created by output caps. Goyal & Ray, *Mind the Cap: Output-Budget Regimes Change the Measured Multilingual Reasoning Gap*, arXiv:2608.04160 (2026), is direct general prior art.

If confirmed here, the narrower contribution is a tuteo/voseo generation-length/truncation interaction inside a deterministic executable-agent evaluation, alongside the clean interface/order factorial.
